# Editing Copilot 1.0 — Quick start

1. **Install** – double-click `Install.cmd` (once; re-run any time to upgrade). It installs everything for your user, free.
2. **Start** – double-click **Editing Copilot** on the Desktop. The setup wizard opens the first time:
   pick your editor, keep **Auto** mode, press **Test** and say "copilot play", choose beeps or spoken replies, Finish.
3. **Open your editor** (Kdenlive or DaVinci Resolve) and talk. Always start with **"copilot"**:
   - "copilot play" · "copilot cut" · "copilot marker here" · "copilot undo"
   - "copilot click the Edit tab" · "copilot open File menu" · "copilot drag Intro to Video 2" · "copilot type Final cut"
   - Kdenlive: "copilot add effect Gaussian blur" · "copilot switch to color workspace" · "copilot open render dialog"
   - Resolve: "copilot go to color page" · "copilot add serial node" · "copilot show scopes"
   - "copilot how do I match skin tones"
4. **Safety** – anything destructive (delete, render, overwrite, project settings, reset) waits until you say **"copilot confirm"** (or "cancel").
   Emergency stop: **Ctrl+Shift+Space** or "copilot stop".
5. **Help** – Tools › *What can I say?* lists every command. The small overlay (bottom-right) shows Listening / Heard / Result; right-click hides it.
6. **Your own shortcuts** – "copilot record macro my export", do the steps, "copilot stop recording"; later just say "copilot my export".

Tips: Kdenlive project edits (guides/splits/trims) are written to a copy – save in Kdenlive first. Resolve free works with all voice/click commands; the external timeline-inspect mode needs Resolve Studio. Logs: `%LOCALAPPDATA%\EditingCopilot\data\logs`.
